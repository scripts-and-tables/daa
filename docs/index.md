# Data Analytics Academy

A **one-year, structured meta-course** for learning data analytics from scratch.

It is a *meta*-course because most of the actual teaching is done by other people: Microsoft Learn, Khan Academy, StatQuest, Coursera, well-known books, YouTube channels, and vendor certifications. This site curates that material, sequences it sensibly, and adds notes from someone walking the same path.

If you are starting from zero and want a clear answer to *"what should I study this week?"*, start here.

---

## How the academy is organised

The course is split into **12 monthly themes**, each broken into **4 weekly topics** sized for roughly **6 hours of study per week** (~300 hours over the year).

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

## Where to start

1. Read [How to use this site](about/how-to-use.md) — five minutes.
2. Skim the [Learning philosophy](about/learning-philosophy.md) — why active recall and spaced repetition matter more than the content itself.
3. Open the [12-month roadmap](roadmap/index.md) and decide whether to follow it linearly or jump to a track.
4. Begin [Month 1 — Foundations](curriculum/month-01-foundations/index.md).

!!! note "Status"

    Month 1 is fully populated. Months 2–12 are stubbed with their themes and sub-topics; full content lands in subsequent passes. The roadmap won't change much — only the depth of each leaf.
