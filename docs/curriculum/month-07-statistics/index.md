## Theme

The deeper version of [Week 4 — Stats intuition](../month-01-foundations/week-04-stats-intuition.md). By the end of this month you can defend a finding statistically and read an A/B test report without missing a trick.

## Weekly plan

| Week  | Topic                                    | Outcome                                                              |
|-------|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 25    | Descriptive statistics — deep dive       | Distributions, moments, quantiles, robust statistics.                 |
| 26    | Sampling, CLT, confidence intervals      | Compute and interpret a 95% CI for a proportion and a mean.           |
| 27    | Hypothesis testing                       | t-test, chi-square, ANOVA — when each applies; p-values honestly.     |
| 28    | Correlation, causation, A/B testing      | Design and read an A/B test; recognise common pitfalls.               |

## Prerequisites

## Mini-project

Find a public A/B-test write-up (e.g. Booking.com, Netflix, Spotify experiments blogs). Re-derive the conclusion: compute the test statistic and CI yourself in Python. Decide whether you agree with the published claim.
