# Books

The books cited across the curriculum, in the order they first become useful.

## Foundations & thinking

- *How to Lie with Statistics* — Darrell Huff. Tiny, old, still the best chapter-per-fallacy tour. **Used in:** [Month 1, Week 3](../curriculum/month-01-foundations/week-03-math-refresher.md).
- *Naked Statistics* — Charles Wheelan. Friendly descriptive stats. **Used in:** [Month 1, Week 4](../curriculum/month-01-foundations/week-04-stats-intuition.md) and [Month 7](../curriculum/month-07-statistics/index.md).
- *Make It Stick* — Brown, Roediger, McDaniel. The cognitive-science case for spaced repetition and active recall. **Used in:** [Learning philosophy](../about/learning-philosophy.md).
- *Decisive* — Chip & Dan Heath. The WRAP framework for decisions. **Used in:** [Month 1, Week 2](../curriculum/month-01-foundations/week-02-problem-framing.md).
- *Thinking in Systems* — Donella Meadows. Mental models for non-linear problems. **Optional, Month 1.**

## Visualization & storytelling

- *Storytelling with Data* — Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic. The de-facto modern reference. **Used in:** [Month 1, Week 1](../curriculum/month-01-foundations/week-01-data-literacy.md) (chapter 1) and [Month 5](../curriculum/month-05-visualization-powerbi/index.md) (full).
- *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* — Edward Tufte. The classic. **Used in:** [Month 1, Week 4](../curriculum/month-01-foundations/week-04-stats-intuition.md) and [Month 5](../curriculum/month-05-visualization-powerbi/index.md).
- *Resonate* / *slide:ology* — Nancy Duarte. Presentation craft. **Used in:** [Month 12](../curriculum/month-12-capstone-soft-skills/index.md).

## Communication

- *The Pyramid Principle* — Barbara Minto. Structured business communication. **Used in:** [Month 1, Week 2](../curriculum/month-01-foundations/week-02-problem-framing.md) (intro) and [Month 12](../curriculum/month-12-capstone-soft-skills/index.md) (full).

## Data warehousing & engineering

- *The Data Warehouse Toolkit* — Ralph Kimball. The dimensional-modelling canon. **Used in:** [Month 8](../curriculum/month-08-data-modeling-warehousing/index.md).
- *Fundamentals of Data Engineering* — Joe Reis & Matt Housley. Modern data-engineering overview. **Used in:** [Month 9](../curriculum/month-09-etl-elt-pipelines/index.md).

## SQL

- *SQL for Data Analysis* — Cathy Tanimura. Practical SQL, organised by analytical pattern. **Used in:** [Months 3–4](../curriculum/month-03-sql-fundamentals/index.md).

## Python & ML

- *Python for Data Analysis* — Wes McKinney (the pandas author). **Used in:** [Month 6](../curriculum/month-06-python-pandas/index.md).
- *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow* — Aurélien Géron. **Used in:** [Month 11](../curriculum/month-11-ml-fundamentals/index.md).

## Optional / canon

- *The Lady Tasting Tea* — David Salsburg. Narrative history of statistics. Optional.
- *DAMA-DMBOK* — encyclopedic data-management reference. Skim only.
