# Practice datasets

Where to find data to practise on. The shortlist below biases towards datasets that are *interesting* (so you stay engaged) and *clean enough* (so you spend your time on analysis, not janitorial work).

## General-purpose collections

- [**Kaggle Datasets**](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets) — the largest and most browsable collection. Quality varies — bias towards datasets with high upvote counts and active discussion.
- [**Google Dataset Search**](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/) — index of indexes; finds datasets across academic, government, and commercial sources.
- [**data.world**](https://data.world/datasets/open-data) — a curated open-data community.

## Government & official

- [**UK ONS**](https://www.ons.gov.uk/) — UK Office for National Statistics. Excellent for socioeconomic time series.
- [**Eurostat**](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat) — pan-European statistics; comparable across EU member states.
- [**US data.gov**](https://www.data.gov/) — large, varied, sometimes scruffy.
- [**World Bank Open Data**](https://data.worldbank.org/) — country-level macroeconomic indicators.

## SQL sandboxes

- [**StrataScratch (free tier)**](https://www.stratascratch.com/) — interview-style SQL exercises with built-in sandbox.
- [**SQLZoo**](https://sqlzoo.net/) — classic browser-based SQL tutorial with built-in tables.
- [**LeetCode (Database)**](https://leetcode.com/problemset/database/) — interview-style, harder questions.
- [**HackerRank SQL**](https://www.hackerrank.com/domains/sql) — graded SQL exercises.

## Sample databases

- **AdventureWorks** — Microsoft's sample relational database, available for SQL Server / Azure SQL. Used widely in PL-300 prep.
- **Sakila** — MySQL/PostgreSQL DVD-rental sample database. Friendly, well-documented schema.
- **Northwind** — old but classic; still common in tutorials.
- [**dbt Labs jaffle_shop**](https://github.com/dbt-labs/jaffle_shop) — small example used in [Month 9](../curriculum/month-09-etl-elt-pipelines/index.md) for dbt exercises.

## Domain-specific & fun

- [**Spotify charts**](https://charts.spotify.com/) — daily/weekly streaming data; great time series.
- [**OpenFlights**](https://openflights.org/data.html) — airlines, airports, routes; classic for joins.
- [**Our World in Data**](https://ourworldindata.org/) — beautifully presented public-interest data with downloadable CSVs.
- [**FBref / Football-Reference**](https://fbref.com/) — football statistics, scrapable, rich.

## A note on scrappy data

Once you finish [Month 6](../curriculum/month-06-python-pandas/index.md) you will be tempted to skip the cleaning practice. Don't. Find one *messy* dataset (ideally one of your own) and spend an afternoon on it. Real-world data is messy; the skill of *making it tidy* is itself the analytical skill.
