<div class="learn-grid" markdown>
<div class="learn-item">
<span class="learn-item__check"></span>
<p class="learn-item__text"><strong>Clean a messy CSV in Excel.</strong> Tables, lookups, conditional aggregation, dates, pivots — the formula library every analyst leans on daily.</p>
</div>
<div class="learn-item">
<span class="learn-item__check"></span>
<p class="learn-item__text"><strong>Query a real database with SQL.</strong> Joins across 4+ tables, CTEs, aggregations, and the empty-string-vs-NULL gotchas that catch most beginners.</p>
</div>
<div class="learn-item">
<span class="learn-item__check"></span>
<p class="learn-item__text"><strong>Reproduce SQL analysis in pandas.</strong> Load, filter, group, and merge with row-count sanity checks — the habits that prevent silent wrong answers.</p>
</div>
<div class="learn-item">
<span class="learn-item__check"></span>
<p class="learn-item__text"><strong>Build a 1-page interactive Power BI dashboard.</strong> Data model, DAX measures, slicers — a deliverable a non-analyst will actually click through.</p>
</div>
<div class="learn-item">
<span class="learn-item__check"></span>
<p class="learn-item__text"><strong>Direct an AI assistant to do real analytics.</strong> The prompting templates, plan mode, and verification habits that make Claude Code a force multiplier instead of a confidently-wrong oracle.</p>
</div>
<div class="learn-item">
<span class="learn-item__check"></span>
<p class="learn-item__text"><strong>Ship a stakeholder-ready 1-page report.</strong> Findings backed by traceable numbers, embedded charts, and recommendations a head of operations can act on.</p>

<span class="section-eyebrow">The capstone</span>
## One question, threaded through every day

One real dataset — Brazilian e-commerce, ~100,000 orders, 2017–2018 — and one business question, threaded through all five days:

> *"Which sellers and product categories are driving low customer satisfaction at Olist, and what's the financial impact?"*

By Friday evening you'll have answered it five different ways and produced a 1-page stakeholder report — with every number traceable to the day you computed it. [See the capstone overview →](capstone/README.md)

<span class="section-eyebrow">Who this is for</span>
## Built for working professionals new to data

- **You've used Excel a handful of times.** That's enough — Day 1 starts from "what's a Table" and builds up.
- **You don't write code yet.** Days 3 and 5 introduce just enough Python and AI tooling to do the work; nothing more.
- **You want a real artifact at the end.** The capstone produces a 1-page report on real data — something you can show colleagues or put in a portfolio.

Not built for: software engineers who already know SQL/pandas (this will feel slow), or someone looking for a 12-week deep dive (this is 5 days, not a bootcamp).
