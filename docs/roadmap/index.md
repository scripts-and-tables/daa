## Suggested cadence

- **6 hours/week** — the default. Hits ~24 hours per month, ~300 hours over the year.
- Treat the month boundary as a checkpoint: did you finish the mini-project? Can you answer the self-check questions without notes? If not, spend an extra week before moving on.

## Outcomes by quarter

- **End of Q1 (Months 1–3).** You can frame an analytical question, clean a spreadsheet, and write reasonable SQL.
- **End of Q2 (Months 4–6).** You can write window functions, build a Power BI dashboard, and replace most spreadsheet work with pandas.
- **End of Q3 (Months 7–9).** You can defend an A/B test result, design a star schema, and reason about ETL pipelines.
- **End of Q4 (Months 10–12).** You can navigate Azure analytics services, evaluate an ML model honestly, and present analytical findings to a non-technical audience. You have a portfolio project and a certification plan.
