## edX

- [Stanford Online — *Databases: Relational Databases and SQL*](https://www.edx.org/learn/relational-databases/stanford-university-databases-relational-databases-and-sql) — *audit free* — used in [Month 3, Week 9](../curriculum/month-03-sql-fundamentals/week-09-relational-model.md). Jennifer Widom's lectures are the gold-standard treatment of relational theory.

## Other browser-based SQL tutorials

- [Mode Analytics — *SQL Tutorial*](https://mode.com/sql-tutorial/) — *free* — used across [Month 3](../curriculum/month-03-sql-fundamentals/index.md). Best free hands-on SQL course on the web; runs in the browser against a startup-style dataset.
- [SQLZoo](https://sqlzoo.net/) — graded browser exercises on a sample database.
- [PostgreSQL Tutorial](https://www.postgresqltutorial.com/) — when you want a PostgreSQL-flavoured second opinion.
